================================================================================
ANNOTATION CLEANUP REPORT
================================================================================

--- OVERVIEW ---
Following the diagnostic analysis of the final_dataset_clean, we identified a critical
contamination issue where 'Notebook' labels were incorrectly being used to bound entire
collections of stationery (including pens, pencils, erasers).

We systematically scanned the dataset for scene-level annotations (bounding box area > 40%)
and quarantined them. We also merged additional high-quality, individually-annotated
stationery items from the source dataset.

--- DELETED / QUARANTINED ANNOTATIONS ---
Class 30 (Notebook):
  - train: 97 contaminated labels removed
  - valid: 12 contaminated labels removed
  - test: 13 contaminated labels removed
  Total: 122 contaminated notebook annotations quarantined.

These annotations incorrectly covered entire desks, meaning pens, pencils, scissors, and
containers were all bounded inside a single 'Notebook' box. They have been moved to:
  final_dataset_clean/quarantined_labels/

--- CORRECTED ANNOTATIONS ---
Imported properly annotated individual stationery objects from source datasets, mapped to
correct class IDs:
  - 231 pen annotations (ID 31)
  - 106 book annotations (ID 29)
  - 255 eraser annotations (ID 33)
  - 212 notebook annotations (ID 30, verified clean)
  - 288 pencil annotations (ID 32)
  - 206 ruler annotations (ID 35)
  - 252 sharpener annotations (ID 34)

--- SUSPICIOUS / MANUAL REVIEW REQUIRED ---
The following files contain annotations that cover a very large area and should be manually reviewed:
  - [train] Furniture Focused In_36541749_7_jpg.rf.33554f6e0e435af7eaba205f35e94e86_4db1aceb8f.txt:2 (near-zero width)
  - [train] Stationary Items Dat_22i-2829_jpg.rf.d7b52ed034a68fad93e8d0d2deca464c_90b6f42a53.txt (notebook aspect ratio 3.5:1)

--- CLASSES AFFECTED ---
Primary affected classes: Notebook (30), Pen (31), Eraser (33)
The contamination severely affected the model's ability to distinguish between pens, erasers,
scissors, and notebooks, because it was trained to view any cluttered desk as a 'Notebook'.
